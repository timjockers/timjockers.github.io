World Robot Olympiad
Team 8-Bit

Together with my teammate Jonas, I returned to the World Robot Olympiad for the 2026 season, competing under the name "Team 8-Bit" in the Junior category. This year marked a significant shift for us as we transitioned to using fischertechnik components to build and program our robot. After securing a strong fourth-place finish at the German National Championship, we qualified for the WRO Open Championship Europe in Zagreb. Although we faced a great deal of bad luck and technical misfortune during the competition rounds, we persevered and ultimately achieved a commendable 19th place out of 49 international teams.
